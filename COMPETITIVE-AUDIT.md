## Where we can win

1. **Decision tools.** Nobody has them. Cost estimator shipped; comparison tool and month-matrix next. On-brand because we are a matching company, not an inventory seller.
2. **Informational terms.** Blogs and Reddit hold "best country for safari" and much of "safari cost" — beatable with better-structured, sourced, dated content.
3. **Radical cost transparency.** Category norm is "enquire for pricing." We show the spread and the exclusions before asking for anything.
4. **Selectivity as position.** Go2Africa covers 15 countries; we cover 4 completely. Framed as a choice, depth beats breadth.

## Where we cannot win yet (owner-gated)

Awards, review volume, financial bonding, B Corp, decades of heritage. Micato's moat is 60 years and ten Travel+Leisure #1 awards — unbuyable. The counter is to compete on axes that do not require time: tooling, transparency, and editorial rigour.

Both address partners/talent/press — never travelers, never commission mechanics.
